Mother wants one of her sons to be the next Duke, and the current one has a week or a month left in him. [[Rittlin]] doesn't want it. So it is probably me, and if it is me, I am going to need someone to do the reading.

## Shopping for an Advisor

I didn't want a clerk. I wanted someone who has been through the grinder and seen the bad and the good, and who has street smarts and a head for books. [[Momen Tomori|Momen]] floated [[Durlan Chaepetor|Durlan]], but he's busy freelancing in [[Atlanta]] and he's a good adventurer, and I'm not stealing him. So I walked the streets of Imfelond as a curious newcomer, which I am, and did not tell a soul the Duke was dying.

Imfelond is too well guarded for proper crime. My sort of contacts here are smugglers and bootleggers, and that's about it. But I learned there's a crop of former guards and port officials who were retired, or sacked for taking bribes. They've run men and they know ledgers and weapons both. The hunting-party leaders down in the southern forest are another option: they're rougher, but they're used to command.

At the docks I tried asking how a fellow might get a "special shipment" through quietly. The dockhands looked at me like I'd asked to borrow their mothers and closed up tight. Fair enough. In a town this full of guards I wouldn't vouch for me either. So I stopped asking, found a quiet corner and listened. Two names kept coming up in low voices: **Tarod** and **Saldonna**. They aren't criminals, exactly, but they move a great deal of cargo, with clerks ticking off ledgers on one side and plenty of muscle on the other. That evening I followed their dockhands into one of the shadier taverns by the water. The talk there was that Saldonna rules hard, prefers muscle and greases wheels to get things done. Tarod is more upstanding. His clerks are better and his men are harder to bribe. I wrote both names down. I'm not hiring anyone yet, because I'm not the Duke yet. But I'll check on them when I'm next in town.

## What Imfelond Thinks

The jeweller's crowd and [[Spesof Beegstick|Spesof]]'s tavern crowd together gave us a fair picture. Folk are mostly content with the Duke and Duchess and they understand why there are so many soldiers, since the town sits between [[Drelia]] and [[The Kingdom of Torveldus|Torveldus]]. They like [[The Empire of Datrus|Datrus]] well enough and are wary of Torveldus. The aristocrats are harsher about it. They call the Torveldans barbarians and blame them for the [[Minamata Malady]]. There are three camps:

- most of high society backs the way things are;
- about one in five nobles are **isolationists**, who think Datrus is as violent as Torveldus and want the food trade cut off;
- a small **United Luachia Party** dreams of joining every kingdom in [[Luachia]] together in peace. Good luck to them.

Down among the commoners the isolationists are nearly as strong as the loyalists. The common folk think the malady is a curse, like a werewolf's bite, and know only that it came from somewhere that isn't elven. It's hitting the poor far harder than the rich, which isn't helping. I said the options were to turn the factions on each other or kill them all. Spesof tells me that isn't statecraft.

## Momen's Errand

Momen reminded us that [[Aemmin Simserion|Aemmin]] beat us to his phylactery and is now gathering ingredients for his ritual. We can't stand watch in every bush in Luachia, but the best place to catch him is a **spore of Moander**, a rotting mass of plant matter soaked in the power of a god of decay. The priests in a soldiers' town won't know anything about that, so our lead points inland to Ellena Edhil, in the swamp. Momen then spent a small fortune on a diamond and had the jeweller grind it to powder. The elf looked down his nose at a dwarf until he decided that dwarves are the only other people who properly appreciate stones. The shop will also make me a new signet ring for six gold, if Mother approves.

Dinner was all vegetables: squash with grapes, which shouldn't work and does, and baked peppers with cranberry. There was also moon juice, a pale, faintly sparkling liqueur the monks of [[Elvenset]] brew. Elves don't hunt for meat. They only hunt monsters that threaten the woods.

## The Blight Hunt

Which is why, at half six the next morning, I joined a hunt on the south side. The quarry was a **Gulthias blight**. Eldrian, who led it, told us the story. An ancient vampire was killed with a stake through the heart, and the stake took root and grew, drinking blood and fouling the land. It treats every living thing as a servant or as fertiliser. That's a lot of evil if even the weapon that killed you goes bad.

It hides as a tree, so the plan was to whack trees one by one until one objected. Momen's magic couldn't find a thing he'd never laid eyes on, so I unrolled my carpet and hovered overhead to watch, and Spesof did the whacking. On the third tree a twig blight dropped out, and a knight split it before it could blink. Once Momen had seen one up close, he could point out every other twig in the area, and we cleared them out. Through Momen's mind link, Eldrian asked whether I'd seen anything move. I had. Momen threw fire at the tree I pointed to. A needle blight screamed and peppered him, and the knights cut it down. A vine blight showed itself next, and I put a single arrow through it.

Then the tree right beside Spesof moved.

A root burst out of the ground, coiled round him and began drinking. It didn't just wound him, it pulled something out of him that doesn't come back with a bandage. A great limb clubbed Momen twice as he backed away, hard enough to rattle his spells loose. The knights closed in from both sides while Eldrian rallied them, and the scouts' arrows mostly bounced off. Then the thing noticed me. Being on a carpet against a clear blue sky turns out to be less hidden than I thought. Its first volley of thorns missed. Then it uprooted itself and lurched toward me, dragging Spesof along in its roots and trampling a knight flat, and its second volley found me. Spesof hacked at it from inside its grip. The trampled knight got up, gulped a potion and went back in swinging.

That's where we are: a walking vampire tree, Spesof still in its roots, Momen battered, and me full of thorns.
