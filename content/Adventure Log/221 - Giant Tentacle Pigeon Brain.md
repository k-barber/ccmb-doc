Tittlin's Log

After the battle with the clump of zombies, we decide to keep heading into the place. We are met with a choice of going through a Curtain or a Door and decide for the Curtain due to its uncontainable nature. Around the corner we find a corridor with a bunch of doors, presumably small rooms on the otherside of each one of them. Upon investigating further I identified which rooms and sounds and which rooms did not have sounds. We start opening the doors one by one dealing with them all. A few of them had some skeletons in them which were easy to counter, and they all had little nick nacks and paddy wacks and such which we collected.

We move forward in the dungeon proceeding through thee next curtain to find a group of 4 Wights. The fight starts while I am in the back of the hallway so I need to haul my ass over to the other side in order to be able to see. I heard what seems to be spesof getting mildly beat up on the other side of the curtain which blinds me from the fight. We manage to go through 2 of them fairly easily but 1 then takes the opportunity to open the door directly behind him which hides an Owlbear. With ease we finish the rest of the Wights and are left with the Owlbear. Spesof backs away from the Owlbear and it seems that it is refusing to leave the room. Momen fears the owlbear and spesof runs in to check the room. Frustrated by only finding food items in the room he turns around and slaughtered the owlbear. Spesof opens the room adjacent to find a kitchen filled with zombies and closes the door immediately.

We decide to check out another door across the hallway and find a Bodak also cooking food. This time we decide to deal with it. Inside the room was a single jar of goo that is called "Mind Nutrients", this was after we defeated the bodak swiftly. We then look further down the hallway to find another way into the power room other than the kitchen and find an area in front of a large door guarded by a bunch of skeletons. After Defeating the skeletons momen and I walk into the room to find a swarm of bones that immediately hit me and grapples momen. Spesof runs up to hit the swarm and some how crits momen in the process brutally injuring him, but also manages to graze the swarm as well slightly damaging it. Ghurde did something to the swarm that made it run away into the next room. [[Ghurde Gorepunch]] lets us know that we should be trying to kill it within the next minute while it cant attack us. We head on into the next room to find this weird Giant Brained Tenticaled with a Beak thingy on a tray and its very weird. The Swarm is also in the back corner and we start dealing damage to it immediately and killing it swiftly.

Spesof decides that it is a good idea to feed the brain the brain nutrients we found earlier, feeding a little bit of it at first and then all of it. The brain does seem to be coming alive after finishing the nutrients. The brain seems to be the control panel (sort of) to the whole facility and lets us know that the artifact we are looking for does exist within the facility with one of the priests and where to get it. Wondering over to the room we get ambushed by a flame skull outside of the room we were looking for. There is also a vampire spawn waiting for us and we deal with those swiftly. We search the room to find some gold and another door. We prep to open the other door and upon doing so find a couple of Vampire Spawns, one of which jumps out to try and scare spesof but I was ready and killed it in one shot. Spesof enters the room further to deal with the other one and another vampire spawn falls on top of him. We deal with the spawns and spesof in a rage opens up the next door and we see a Vampire staring right at us!
