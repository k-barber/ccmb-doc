We left off on the street outside the [[Cathedral of Rejuvenation]], freshly thrown out by its priests, holding a boat over our heads. In our defence, the circle there only goes one way, and the river does the rest.

## The Duke and the General

Before the boat went in the water, [[Momen Tomori|Momen]] decided we ought to tell the crown that we were about to stitch the country together with doorways that never close, in the middle of a plague. The [[Alfred Oberstein Ⅲ|Duke]] took it well. He showed us his map of [[Luachia]]: green where the [[Minamata Malady|sickness]] hasn't reached ([[The Imperial Plains]], [[The Great Badger Expanse]], [[Wustus]], and all of [[Tilnola]] and northern [[Spueria]], where it's too cold for it) and red over [[Tunris Valley|Tunris]] and southern Spueria. Put a circle in [[Locust Gate]] and another in [[Hvitsand]], he said, and the cure brewing in his workshop could go through them instead of down a hundred miles of road.

Then he handed us to General Gak, a scarred half-orc built like a gatehouse, who looked us over and said, *Oh, look at you lads there, you're tiny.* He made an exception for [[Spesof Beegstick|Spesof]]. Gak calls himself a backliner and a logistics man. He was glad we hadn't put any circles abroad, and much less glad that ours need no mage to work them, because now any thief who wanders onto the chalk ends up in the capital. He also reminded us that [[Atlanta]] sits barely ten kilometres from [[The Kingdom of Torveldus|Torveldus]], which I had been trying not to think about.

Momen painted the capital's circle in a supply closet ten feet on a side. The door is too narrow for a wagon but wide enough for his floating disc. Meanwhile Gak explained to Spesof how the cure goes out. They keep the crates somewhere safe and survey the town. Anyone in the late stages who can still be saved gets dosed on the spot, the rest are triaged from worst to least, and anyone too far gone is put down. Mages knock people senseless, and men with nets and bolas catch the runners. He said all this as if he were reading a supply list. Then we stepped through to Atlanta, where he and [[Ghurde Gorepunch|Brigadier General Gorepunch]] saluted like old drinking partners. Gak briefed [[Merric Porridgepot|Merric]] and posted men round the site.

## Locust Gate

Locust Gate was quiet in the bad way. Silver-skinned figures ducked behind buildings as we came off the dock. The sickness had beaten us there, teleporting or not. The infected came out to look at us on the walk up to the manor, saw the general's soldiers, and went back inside. Gak hammered on Earl Fabian Schwarzmann's door in the King Consort's name. The Earl was healthy and delighted to see us, since we're the reason he has a wife, and he had already cleared a harbour warehouse for us. Momen spent another hour on his knees, a soldier hopped through and back to call *all clear*, and the general's men poured out into the town. It was twenty to four in the afternoon.

## Kongstead and Hvitsand

From there we carried the boat overland, paddled, then carried it again. We passed Kongstead, which was one of the busiest towns on that river and now looks like a wasteland. The general grimaced and said it would be a priority on the way back, but he could not spare a single crate. Nobody said much for a while.

Hvitsand was better. Either the cold has slowed the sickness or everyone is simply indoors. [[Phillipp Kallestad|Kallestad]] wasn't expecting us so soon, but he cleared some storerooms in half an hour, and by ten at night the antidote was coming through. Gak left us there. There's no plague in Tilnola, so his men are better spent in Spueria and Tunris, Kongstead especially. He'll wait in Atlanta for our next circle to light up.

## North

We sailed overnight round the tip of Spueria to Trondkan, with the skeleton crew rowing while we slept. Someone reminded me that the dog-thing we caged is stored in there with them. It is not my pet. I was specifically told it was not my pet. It will be fed tonight. We hired a cart for one gold piece rather than roll the barrel of teleportation fluid through the snow, and reached [[Nørreholte]] just before noon.

That was when it emerged that we have no deal with the Marquis. None. We told the Duke we did. We put Nørreholte on the list because it seemed a nice place for a circle. We don't talk about the deal.

While we waited for a two o'clock appointment, the conversation turned to [[Drelia]], which is badly red on the map. Momen asked how I felt about my kin. I told him I care, but probably not as much as a Drelian should. Perhaps one day I'll conquer it, but not today. With some effort he reached up, put an arm around my shoulders, and told me it was a terrible way to grow up, not knowing your clan. I let him.

Spesof is worried about his family in [[Helsinki]], so he used the sending stone to reach Plock, and through him Gak. On the general's advice, our next circle goes to Marshal's Field in the Scattered Steppe rather than Arrowburn. The river there runs west toward Drelia, and the Drelians stand as a buffer between us and [[Ublijan]], so sending them the cure would buy a lot of goodwill. The town is poorly placed for defence, since Torveldus could sail up that river, but it beats every route west we have now. Gak is also sending a man to check on the Beegsticks.

Lunch was red dragon goulash and baked beholder at two gold apiece, and Spesof says it was the best meat he has ever eaten. Afterwards Momen copied Disintegrate into his spellbook, which I'm sure was unrelated.

## The Marquis

Spesof flattered the Marquis and his cooks, then pitched him the network. The Marquis wanted details. Was this a private enterprise with a toll, or a public good run by the crown? We did not know. We had never once thought about the money. So we worked it out on the spot. The network runs out of Atlanta under Sir Merric, and there will be fees eventually. Travel is free while the cure is moving, and proper terms come later. If he ever dislikes the arrangement, he can take a broom to the chalk. He'll put it to his advisors and answer us tomorrow.

On the way out I suggested that next time we simply put something in his pocket. I have paid people off before. It worked.

We slept in Nørreholte while Momen copied spells late into the night. It is now the first of Conmac. There are circles in the capital, Atlanta, Locust Gate and Hvitsand, the cure is moving, and Marshal's Field is next. Meanwhile we wait to hear whether a man who doesn't know what we're charging will let us draw on his floor.
