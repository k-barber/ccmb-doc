A comital family (count) from [[Tillnola]]. They control the south-eastern portion of Tillnola, including Trondkan and Ålekjer.

They are a proud group of warriors and keep the many monster nests in their territory under control with ease.

They fought valiantly during the [[Datrian-Torveldan War]] and were expected to receive a parcel of land in the [[The Great Badger Expanse]] for their contributions. Instead, the [[Rian Wyrmwood|Wyrmwood]] family, a much smaller family was granted land. This was especially insulting considering that  Rian Wyrmwood was a known coward, who was expected to die in the war.

This surprise has led many to believe that the Wyrmwoods had some sort of shady deal with the imperial family.

They kidnapped [[Katya Wyrmwood]], and are now causing a fuss.
